# CareBridge AI (Voice-to-Report Caregiver Documentation Assistant)

## Vision
CareBridge AI is an AI administrative assistant designed for in-home caregivers, private aides, and small home care agencies. It bridges the gap between raw voice documentation during fast-paced care shifts and the structured, compliance-heavy administrative artifacts required by clinical teams, Medicaid/insurance billing bodies, and family members.

## Target Audience
- In-home caregivers and private nursing aides needing rapid shift documentation.
- Small home care agencies seeking streamlined, compliant documentation workflows.
- Family members of care recipients who need transparent, accessible updates on loved one well-being.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Fast Voice Ingestion**: Caregivers record a quick 45-second voice note post-shift.
- **Structured Clinical Shift Log**: Automatically extracts medications, mobility tracking, nutrition intake, and vital signs into standard medical logs.
- **Medicaid/Insurance Billing Note**: Generates compliant billing summary notes for reimbursement.
- **Warm Family Update**: Translates clinical shift details into an empathetic, layperson-friendly family update.

## Key Features & Workflow
1. **Voice Ingestion & Cleaning**: Handles voice note processing, transcription, and noise removal.
2. **Clinical Metric & Metric Extraction**: Extracts vitals, medications, mobility notes, and dietary records.
3. **Empathetic Summary Generation**: Translates technical shift notes into a warm summary for family members.
4. **REST API Gateway & Dashboard Interface**: Go backend for API endpoints and mobile-first frontend.
