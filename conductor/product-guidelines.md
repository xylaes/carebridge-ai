# Product Guidelines: CareBridge AI

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Empathetic & Warm**: When presenting updates to family members, use comforting, clear, non-jargon language.
- **Clinical & Accurate**: When generating Clinical Care Shift Logs and Medicaid billing notes, use precise, professional clinical terminology.
- **Supportive & Efficient**: For caregiver interfaces, prioritize clarity, speed, and low cognitive burden after long shifts.

## Design & UX Principles
- **Mobile-First Efficiency**: Optimized for quick one-tap voice recordings on mobile devices.
- **Visual Hierarchy & Accessibility**: High contrast, legible typography (e.g. Inter / Roboto), and distinct visual markers for vitals and alerts.
- **Glassmorphism & Warm Palette**: Modern aesthetic featuring calm blues, warm teals, and soft dark mode elements.

## Quality & Compliance Standards
- **Data Confidentiality**: Maintain strict standards for patient privacy and health data handling.
- **Auditability**: Ensure generated clinical logs preserve raw caregiver notes for traceability.
